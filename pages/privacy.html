THE PULSE

Privacy Policy

This summary describes the current project behavior. Review it for your jurisdiction and hosting setup before public launch.

Information stored

Account records include your name, email address, and a bcrypt password hash. The site stores a session identifier in a browser cookie. Newsletter signups store an email address. Contact messages store the name, email address, subject, and message you submit.

News requests and external links

News searches are sent to GNews through the site server. Opening a story takes you to a third-party publisher, which applies its own privacy practices.

Storage and retention

These records are stored in the site's SQLite database. The site owner controls the host, database retention, and backups. This project currently has no self-service account, newsletter, or message deletion controls.

Cookies

The login session cookie is used to keep you signed in. It is HTTP-only and uses same-site protection; on production, it is configured as secure for HTTPS.

Privacy requests

Use the contact form for a data or privacy request. Do not include your password in a message.
